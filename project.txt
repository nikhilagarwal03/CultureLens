Project Documentation
CultureLens (Working Name)
Understanding Global Internet Culture Through Local Context
________________________________________
1. Project Overview
The internet connects people globally, but cultural context remains local. Many users encounter memes, slang, traditions, or trends from other cultures that are difficult to understand.
CultureLens is an AI-powered platform that explains cultural references, internet trends, memes, and social phenomena by:
•	Identifying their origin
•	Explaining their cultural significance
•	Translating them into local cultural analogies
•	Delivering the explanation in the user’s preferred language
The platform bridges the gap between global internet culture and local cultural understanding.
________________________________________
2. Problem Statement
The internet is global, but cultural understanding is fragmented.
Users frequently encounter references they cannot fully understand, such as:
•	international memes
•	cultural traditions
•	viral trends
•	internet slang
•	pop culture references
Examples:
•	Why is the Super Bowl such a big deal in the US?
•	What does “senpai” mean in anime culture?
•	Why is the Roman Empire meme trending?
•	Why do Japanese fans line up overnight for anime merchandise?
Traditional translation tools only translate language, but they do not translate culture.
This leads to misunderstanding or loss of context.
________________________________________
3. Solution
CultureLens provides AI-powered cultural interpretation.
Given any input (word, phrase, question, or paragraph), the system:
1.	Detects the core cultural reference
2.	Identifies the origin culture
3.	Explains its meaning and context
4.	Generates a local cultural analogy
5.	Localizes the explanation into the user’s language
Example:
Input:
Why is the Super Bowl so popular?
Output:
Reference
The Super Bowl is the championship game of the National Football League (NFL) in the United States.
Cultural Impact
It is one of the most watched television events in the world and a major cultural event in the US.
Local Analogy (India)
Its cultural importance is similar to the IPL final combined with a major festival-like event.
